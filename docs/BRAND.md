# Brand

> Farmers know their machines. Engineers know their trade. The brand's job is to show, in every screen and sentence, that **we know it too**, and that when we don't, we'll get you someone who does.

## 1. Positioning

**For** UK agricultural machinery dealers and the farmers and engineers they serve,
**[Brand]** is the parts and service platform that **gets the right part and the right procedure to the machine, first time**.
**Unlike** dealer systems built for the back office, it is built for the yard, the field and the counter: photo in, right part out; the manufacturer's procedure on the engineer's phone, even without signal.
**Because** it is grounded in the dealer's own data and licensed manufacturer content, checked by the dealer's own people, and it hands over to a human the moment it isn't sure.

Proof points we'll earn in the pilot: % right part first time, callback-within-the-hour rate, number of verified procedures, zero spec errors.

## 3. Brand rules that matter in agriculture

1. **Never borrow OEM colours or language.** Deere green and yellow, Massey and Case reds, New Holland blue, CLAAS and Fendt greens, Kubota orange, JCB yellow are off-limits for brand use. We serve every make, and dealers are franchise-bound. Our accent is **teal**, a colour no major ag OEM owns.
2. **Safety colours mean safety.** Red, amber, blue and green are used only with their ISO 3864 meanings (danger, caution, instruction, safe condition). Never for decoration or marketing.
3. **The dealer comes first.** In customer-facing screens the dealer's name and logo lead; we're "powered by". Farmers trust *their* dealer.
4. **Numbers are sacred.** Part numbers and specs are set in a monospace face exactly as the source writes them, with the page reference beside them. Never rounded, restyled or paraphrased.
5. **Show the evidence.** Every answer carries its reason: the photo match, the fitment line, the manual page.

## 4. Voice

**Sound like the best parts person or foreman you've ever dealt with:** knows the machine, talks straight, gives you the number, admits when they need to check, and rings back when they say they will.

| Principle | Instead of | Say |
|---|---|---|
| Specific beats clever | "Our AI-powered solution found matches!" | "That's an oil filter for your 6150R. Number RE504836, superseded by RE539279. Straight swap." |
| Respect their knowledge | "A filter is a component that…" | "Inner or outer element?" |
| Honest when unsure | "Sorry, an error occurred." | "I can't be certain from that photo. Dave in parts will ring you before 3:15." |
| Real people, real times | "A ticket has been raised." | "Sarah's got your photos and will call you within the hour." |
| Plain British English | "Leverage our omnichannel platform" | "Send a photo. We'll find the part." |
| No fake urgency | "Only 2 left, buy now!" | "2 at Hereford, 5 at Ludlow. Collect today or next-day to the farm." |
| Safety without drama | "WARNING!!!" | "Lower the head and take the key out first. It runs on for a minute." |

Never say "AI-powered" to farmers. Say what it does. (Fine in dealer and investor material.)

## 5. Visual identity

### Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `ink` | `#15191C` | Primary text, dark surfaces, spec chips (stamped-metal feel) |
| `mist` | `#EEF1EE` | Light backgrounds: cool, slightly green-grey, easy in sunlight (avoids the warm-cream look) |
| `steel` | `#5E6A71` | Secondary text, borders, icons |
| `teal-700` | `#0A6E69` | **Brand accent**: primary buttons, links, active states (on mist) |
| `teal-400` | `#2BB3A8` | Accent on dark surfaces, focus rings |
| `loam` | `#6B5440` | Earth accent for illustration and marketing only |
| `signal-danger` | `#C62828` | Danger / stop (safety only) |
| `signal-caution` | `#F2A900` | Caution / check (safety only) |
| `signal-ok` | `#2E7D32` | Done / in stock / safe |

Dark mode is first-class (cabs at night, workshops): ink backgrounds, mist text, teal-400 accent.

### Type (all open licence, free)

- **Archivo** (semi-expanded, 600–800): wordmark, headings. Industrial grotesk with a nod to machine plates.
- **Inter**: interface and body text.
- **IBM Plex Mono**: part numbers, serials, specs, page references.

### Logo direction (Linchpin)

A wordmark in Archivo Expanded, **linchpin**, lower case, where the dot of the *i* is the linch pin's spring hoop: a ring sitting on the stem. It reduces to a symbol (ring + stem) for app icons and favicons. Teal symbol, ink wordmark on mist; mist on ink reversed.

### Imagery

Real dealers, real yards, real mud, real hands. Engineers at machines, parts counters, phones held in work gloves. **No** stock "smiling farmer with tablet", **no** glossy OEM livery dominating a shot, **no** drones-over-wheat clichés.

### Interface principles

- Gloves and sunlight: targets ≥ 48px, contrast ≥ 4.5:1, mist not pure white.
- Numbers first: part number and price are the biggest things on a part card.
- One clear next action per screen.
- The original manual page is always one tap away.
